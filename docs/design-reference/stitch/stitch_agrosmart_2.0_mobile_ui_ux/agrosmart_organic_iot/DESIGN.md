## Brand & Style

This design system embodies an organic, premium modern AgriTech aesthetic tailored specifically for Android handheld devices in varied field environments. It serves small-scale farmers cultivating high-yield vegetable plots and paddy fields, bridging raw agronomy with precision IoT automation.

The visual direction merges **Organic Minimalism** with tactile, field-ready environmental design. It departs from sterile, industrial enterprise software by grounding every screen in warm soil, deep canopy greens, and ambient outdoor light. The interface balances high-density agronomic data—soil moisture, reservoir levels, NPK ratios, and microclimate telemetry—with soft botanical curves, breathing space, and immediate legibility under direct sunlight.

The emotional goal is reassurance, vitality, and precision stewardship: a digital companion that feels as dependable as seasoned farming tools, yet as sophisticated as top-tier modern instrumentation.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in an earthy, high-chroma chlorophyll scale complemented by natural mineral neutrals. The canvas avoids stark artificial whites, opting instead for sun-warmed cream and vegetal shades that reduce glare during high-noon field operations.

### Palette Implementation
- **Primary Canopy Green (`#183D2B`)**: Dominates high-impact brand elements, key actionable buttons, Android top app bar titles, active navigation states, and primary section headers.
- **Secondary Foliage Green (`#387A4B`)**: Highlights interactive toggles, healthy status badges, positive delta metrics, and secondary action surfaces.
- **Fresh Growth Green (`#6D9B62`)**: Drives active progress rings, pump activation states, healthy sensor nodes, and dynamic chart curves.
- **Sage Mist (`#A8B99A`)**: Soft structural framing, inactive track indicators, and gentle dividing accents.
- **Pale Sprout (`#E8EFE2`)**: Subtle component fills, container chips, secondary pill buttons, and highlight overlays.
- **Cream Earth Base (`#F6F4EA`)**: Global screen backdrop; creates a soft organic paper feel that eliminates harsh contrast outdoors.
- **Warm White Cards (`#FFFEF9`)**: The primary elevated surface for modular data cards, sensor metrics, and bottom sheets.
- **Earth Loam Brown (`#987451`)**: Soil condition telemetry, root-zone sensor markers, compost statuses, and terrestrial milestones.
- **Canal Water Blue (`#559FC1`)**: Irrigation toggles, valve controllers, rainfall tracking, and water table level meters.
- **Text & Structure**: Primary readability is commanded by Deep Chlorophyll (`#26382B`) for maximum outdoor contrast, supported by Secondary Stem Gray (`#778277`) for telemetry labels, timestamps, and metric units. Structural divisions utilize Botanical Border (`#E3E7DC`).
- **Telemetry Alerts**: Sunlight Yellow (`#E5B94D`) for dry-soil/depleted nutrient cautions, and Soil Alert Red (`#D65A4A`) for critical frost, valve failure, or severe desiccation warnings.

## Typography

The type system is powered exclusively by **Plus Jakarta Sans**, chosen for its geometric foundation, wide apertures, clean terminals, and humanist curves that retain high legibility under direct glare.

### Typographic Hierarchy & Field Utility
- **Sensor Metric Displays (`metric-xl`, `metric-lg`)**: Numerical data is set with deliberate boldness and tightened tracking. Numbers must read instantly from arm's length while a farmer stands over a paddy ridge or vegetable nursery.
- **Section Headers (`headline-lg`, `headline-md`)**: Styled with `#183D2B` to establish anchor points on the screen, creating clear visual resting spots between data widgets.
- **Telemetry Labels (`label-md`, `label-sm`)**: Set in semi-bold uppercase or prominent title case using `#778277` with extended tracking (+0.04em to +0.06em) to differentiate units of measurement (e.g., `% VWC`, `pH`, `EC mS/cm`, `°C`) from raw values.
- **Reflow & Mobile Adaptability**: When screens shrink or density demands spike, large hero figures reflow cleanly using `metric-xl-mobile`, preventing line wrapping inside multi-column field cards.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built for native Android ergonomics, engineered around a single-handed, thumb-driven interaction zone across compact, medium, and expansive Android form factors.

### Grid & Canvas Structure
- **Columns & Margins**: A 4-column fluid layout with an outer canvas boundary of `1.25rem` (`20px`) and an inter-card `gutter` of `1rem` (`16px`). For narrower Android devices (<360dp width), margins drop seamlessly to `margin-compact` (`1rem`).
- **Touch Target Fidelity**: All touch targets for valve actions, sensor filters, and zone switches adhere strictly to a minimum physical bounding box of 48×48dp, preventing mis-taps when hands are gloved or wet.
- **Vertical Rythm**: Component sections stack using `space-lg` (`1.25rem`) to maintain distinct separation between irrigation controls, environmental readings, and weather alerts.
- **Android Safe Zones**: Top margins account for edge-to-edge camera cutouts/notches with a minimum safe inset of 24dp above app bar content. Bottom layouts preserve 80dp of clear buffer to accommodate the fixed Android Bottom Navigation Bar and Android gesture pill.

## Elevation & Depth

This system intentionally rejects synthetic, black-tinted drop shadows in favor of **Natural Botanical Diffusion** and **Layered Organic Substrates**.

### Depth Layers
1. **Field Ground (Base 0dp)**: Rendered in Cream Background (`#F6F4EA`). This is the lowest structural plane.
2. **Elevated Substrate (Cards 1dp - 2dp)**: Warm White Cards (`#FFFEF9`) feature a 1px continuous hairline outline using `#E3E7DC` paired with a diffused ambient shadow:
   - `box-shadow: 0 4px 16px -2px rgba(24, 61, 43, 0.05), 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(24, 61, 43, 0.03);`
   - The slight forest-green tint in the shadow creates an optical tie to outdoor leaf scatter and tree shade.
3. **Floating Controls & Modals (4dp - 8dp)**: Quick irrigation toggles, floating action buttons (FABs), and bottom configuration sheets lift off the canvas with a deeper forest drop:
   - `box-shadow: 0 10px 24px -4px rgba(24, 61, 43, 0.12), 0 3px 6px -1px rgba(24, 61, 43, 0.06);`
4. **Moisture Gauges & Data Glass**: Inset metric gauges (such as reservoir or water tank depth) use a debossed internal soft shadow (`inset 0 2px 4px rgba(24, 61, 43, 0.06)`) paired with water-tone gradients (`#559FC1` to `#6D9B62`) to evoke physical liquid containment.

## Shapes

The design system uses soft, organic curvature reflecting leaf nodes, water droplets, and terraced fields. Sharp corners are entirely absent from primary containers.

### Shape Hierarchy
- **Primary Container Cards**: Employs `rounded-xl` (`1.5rem` / `24px`) to `rounded-2xl` (`2rem` / `32px`) on parent cards, sensor containers, and telemetry clusters.
- **Interactive Action Elements**: Buttons, search inputs, and valve controls utilize full pill forms (`rounded-full` / `9999px`) to emphasize tap accessibility and organic flow.
- **Embedded Badges & Status Chips**: Radius of `0.75rem` (`12px`) for status indicators, preventing tension with the larger curved parent containers.
- **Sheet Corners**: Bottom modal sheets and drawer panels feature top-edge radii of `2rem` (`32px`), creating a natural nested cradle for one-handed operation.

## Components

### 1. Navigation & App Chrome
- **Android Status Bar**: Rendered with light system icons or dark tinted icons on `#F6F4EA` edge-to-edge transparent integration.
- **Bottom Navigation Bar**: Fixed warm white dock (`#FFFEF9`) with top border `#E3E7DC`. Holds 5 primary destinations: **Home**, **Farm**, **Irrigation**, **Analytics**, and **Profile**.
  - *Active Item*: Solid `#183D2B` pill container with `#E8EFE2` accent background wrap or an illuminated Fresh Green dot indicator below the icon.
  - *Inactive Items*: Minimal stroke icons set in `#778277` with `label-sm` text.

### 2. Buttons & Actions
- **Primary Field Action**: `#183D2B` background with `#FFFEF9` text. Rounded-full, 52px height for primary mobile triggers (e.g., "Start Irrigation Cycle"). Soft green shadow on press.
- **Secondary Action**: `#E8EFE2` fill with `#183D2B` label text. Borderless and non-distracting.
- **Emergency / Stop Action**: `#D65A4A` fill with `#FFFEF9` text; reserved for manual emergency solenoid shutoff.

### 3. Metric Gauges & Sensor Cards
- **Vegetable & Paddy Moisture Cards**: `#FFFEF9` background, `rounded-2xl`, bordered with `#E3E7DC`. 
- **Circular Moisture Gauge**: An SVG/Canvas radial track in `#E8EFE2` with a dynamic fill stroke in `#559FC1` (water) or `#387A4B` (soil moisture). Large `metric-xl` centered digits accompanied by a micro status pill (e.g., "Optimal", "Parched", "Flooded").
- **NPK / Soil Health Bars**: Horizontal dual-tone rounded tracks with segmented indicators displaying Nitrogen, Phosphorus, and Potassium levels against healthy target brackets.

### 4. Interactive Toggles & Irrigation Switchers
- **Solenoid Valve Toggle**: A tactile switch measuring 64×36dp. When active, the background transitions to `#559FC1` with a `#FFFEF9` circular thumb displaying an embedded running droplet glyph. Inactive state rests at `#A8B99A`.

### 5. Chips & Filter Tags
- **Plot Selectors (Vegetables vs. Paddy)**: Pill-shaped toggle chips. Unselected chips have transparent backgrounds with a 1.5px `#E3E7DC` border and `#778277` text. Selected chips fill with `#183D2B` and white text, accompanied by an icon representing the crop type.

### 6. Lists & Log Entries
- **Irrigation & Agronomy History**: Grouped rows separated by subtle dividers (`#E3E7DC`). Each item features an icon circle with `#E8EFE2` background housing specific agricultural glyphs (water pump, fertilizer dispersal, sensor ping), primary text in `#26382B`, and timestamps aligned right in `body-sm` `#778277`.

### 7. Form Inputs & Diagnostics
- **Field Data Input**: Background `#FFFEF9` with a persistent 1.5px outline in `#E3E7DC`. On focus, the border shifts to `#387A4B` with an ambient pale sprout glow ring. Placeholder text styled in `#778277`.
